通胀预期
New York Fed respondents kept one-, three- and five-year inflation expectations at 3.9%, 3.3% and 3.0%, while the probability that unemployment will be higher in a year rose 1.4 points to 45.3%, the highest since April 2020. — New York Fed · Nov 9, 11:00 ET
