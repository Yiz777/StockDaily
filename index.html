通胀
October PCE inflation rose 0.3% month over month and 3.8% year over year; core PCE also rose 0.3% monthly and accelerated to 3.3% annually, 0.1 percentage point above the 3.2% consensus. — BEA / AP · 2026-11-25 08:30 ET

增长
Third-quarter real GDP was revised to a 4.3% annual rate from 3.4%, as stronger investment and consumer spending lifted private domestic demand; real final sales to private domestic purchasers rose 5.8%. — BEA · 2026-11-25 08:30 ET
💬 三季度经济增速从3.4%上修到4.3%，不是靠单一项目撑起来，企业投资和居民消费都比第一次统计更强。经济底子好，对公司盈利是好事，但也让美联储更有空间继续加息。
