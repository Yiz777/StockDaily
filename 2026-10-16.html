上游通胀
September final-demand PPI jumped 1.3% month over month versus roughly 0.5% expected and 6.3% year over year; goods prices rose 2.6%, led by a 20.3% gasoline jump, while services increased 0.8%. — U.S. BLS / Reuters · Oct 15, 08:30 ET
💬 工厂和服务商的成本一个月涨1.3%，差不多是市场预期的两倍多；一年涨6.3%。汽油单月涨20.3%，但服务也涨0.8%，说明压力不只来自油价。
→ 企业成本升得更快 → 降息更难、利润率受压 → QQQ/WDC不追涨，现金继续等WDC财报与FOMC。
消费
September retail and food-services sales reached $745.0 billion, up 0.8% month over month versus 0.3% expected and 6.3% year over year; sales excluding gasoline stations rose only 0.2%. — U.S. Census / AP · Oct 15, 08:30 ET
💬 消费看起来很强，月增0.8%，远高于预期0.3%；但剔除加油站后只涨0.2%。也就是说，一部分“多花的钱”只是被更贵的汽油吃掉，不全是大家买得更多。
→ 消费没塌但被能源挤压 → MCD客流仍要看财报 → MCD继续持有，不因一天上涨追买。
就业
Initial unemployment claims were 199,000 in the week ended October 10, up 1,000 from the revised prior week; continuing claims fell 4,000 to 1.710 million. — U.S. DOL · Oct 15, 08:30 ET
