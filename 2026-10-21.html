The federal funds target is 3.75%–4.00% after September's 25-bp hike; 94 of 100 economists polled by Reuters expect no October change, while all 100 expect a December increase. — Reuters · Oct 20, 08:43 ET

住房数据
September housing starts fell 5.2% month over month to a 1.221 million annual rate and were 7.4% below a year earlier; single-family starts dropped 4.6%, while permits slipped 0.2% to 1.400 million. — U.S. Census Bureau · Oct 20, 08:30 ET
💬 新屋开工年化只剩122.1万套，一个月少5.2%，比去年少7.4%；最能代表普通家庭需求的独栋开工也少4.6%。高房贷利率确实把住房压冷了，但油价和工资让Fed还不能立刻转松。

信用与房贷
Equifax Q3 revenue rose 10% to $1.693 billion and net income rose 32% to $211.7 million, while adjusted EPS increased 11% to $2.26; it trimmed its full-year adjusted-EPS midpoint by $0.04 amid softer mortgage activity. — Equifax IR · Oct 20, time not stated
